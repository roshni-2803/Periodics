FAQ & Natural Remedies Guide

Clear answers to your menstrual health, symptoms, and medical queries.

What kind of physical and emotional symptoms are expected during periods?

Common symptoms include lower abdominal cramps (dysmenorrhea), breast tenderness, bloating, fatigue, mood swings, mild headaches, and lower back aches due to uterine contractions and hormonal shifts.

Quick Remedies: Apply a hot water compress or heating pad to your lower abdomen, sip warm ginger or chamomile tea, and practice light stretching or walking.

Should a user consult a doctor if she missed her periods for 3-4 months?

Yes, absolutely. Missing periods for 3 to 4 consecutive months (amenorrhea) outside of pregnancy or breastfeeding is a critical sign that you should consult a gynecologist or doctor for a professional health check-up and hormone evaluation.

How to manage bloating and water retention during cycles?

Hormonal fluctuations cause the body to hold onto extra water. Drinking plenty of pure water (aiming for 2.5L daily), reducing salty and processed foods, and eating potassium-rich foods like bananas help naturally alleviate bloating.

FAQ & Natural Remedies

Understanding physical symptoms and quick soothing techniques.

What causes menstrual cramps (dysmenorrhea)?

Cramps are caused by uterine contractions as the body sheds its lining, triggered by hormone-like substances called prostaglandins. Higher levels lead to stronger cramps.

Quick Remedy: Apply a hot water compress or heating pad to the lower abdomen for 15-20 minutes.

Why do mood swings and fatigue happen before a period?

Fluctuating estrogen and progesterone levels during the luteal phase impact brain neurotransmitters like serotonin, causing mood shifts, irritability, and low energy.

Quick Remedy: Light walking, herbal chamomile tea, and prioritizing 7-8 hours of sleep.
